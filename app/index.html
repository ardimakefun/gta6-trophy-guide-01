Missables — what can actually be missed

Why only one trophy is flagged

There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so the instinct is to worry about everything. In practice almost nothing is missable: story trophies pop automatically as you play, and every optional questline stays available in free-roam after the story — PowerPyx confirms this for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret, and for the Strangers & Freaks strands. Optional missions that disappear (black icons with pink letters) do not matter for trophies, and there are far more than the 100 optional missions the 100% checklist asks for.

Save strategy (cheap insurance)

The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Keep a save at the start of every chapter (5 saves), one before the final mission, and rotate the rest for free-roam cleanup. That covers the one trophy below and any choice you later regret — including the Chapter 1 meditation, which is the only thing in the whole list that can disappear.

Not missable, despite looking like it

  • Story trophies — every chapter and named-mission trophy pops automatically; you cannot skip a chapter.
  • Character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret, Cal) — quest givers can go quiet mid-story but all return and stay available after the story.
  • Kingpin Bowling valuables — the robbery icon respawns, so you can come back for the items you missed.
  • 100% checklist — nothing toward it is missable; everything is doable in free-roam after the story.

What is not yet confirmed

Five trophies have no confirmed unlock method in any published guide yet: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their entries say so plainly and give only what the official description supports. PowerPyx lists all five as currently unknown (with a believed lead for three of them); this guide repeats the lead and labels it as a belief, not a method. Check the PowerPyx link beside each one for updates rather than following an unverified method.
